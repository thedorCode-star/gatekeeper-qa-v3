# Gatekeeper QA Business Foundation

## Vision

To become a trusted B2B Quality Engineering platform that helps startups and growing businesses make better software decisions through reliable quality insights, risk visibility, and actionable reporting—reducing the cost of defects and improving product reliability.

## Mission

Gatekeeper QA helps software teams understand and reduce product quality risks by first learning their product, users, requirements, and critical workflows, then applying exploratory testing and structured quality engineering to identify issues, provide actionable evidence, and support confident release decisions.

## Core Values

### 1. Integrity
Gatekeeper QA reports quality findings truthfully and provides evidence-based recommendations, even when those findings may affect a planned release.

### 2. Risk-Based Quality
Gatekeeper QA prioritizes testing and quality recommendations according to business impact, customer impact, and product risk rather than simply the number of defects found.

### 3. Prevention & Early Quality
Gatekeeper QA promotes quality throughout the software development lifecycle by helping teams prevent and identify problems as early as possible.

### 4. Collaboration
Gatekeeper QA works as a quality partner with developers, product teams, and business stakeholders. Quality is a shared responsibility, and our goal is to help teams release successfully rather than create conflict between QA and development.

### 5. Continuous Improvement
Gatekeeper QA analyzes testing results, defects, release outcomes, and recurring quality problems to identify root causes and help teams continuously improve their development and QA processes.

## Target Customer Profile

### Primary Customer

Gatekeeper QA primarily targets growing B2B SaaS companies that:

- Have approximately 10–50 employees.
- Already have active users or paying customers.
- Release software frequently.
- Have a development team but limited or no dedicated QA capacity.
- Rely heavily on developers to test their own features.
- Experience regression issues or defects reaching customers.
- Need greater confidence before production releases.
- Want to improve software quality without immediately building a large internal QA department.

### Primary Buyer

The primary buyer is the CTO, Head of Engineering, or equivalent technical decision-maker.

Gatekeeper QA helps this person improve visibility into product quality, reduce release risk, protect engineering productivity, and make better release decisions using quality evidence.

### Secondary Stakeholders

Gatekeeper QA may also work closely with:

- Founders and CEOs concerned about customer experience, reputation, and the business cost of software defects.
- Engineering Managers concerned about development productivity, regressions, and release reliability.
- Product Managers concerned about requirements, product behavior, and customer experience.

### Initial Client Approach

Gatekeeper QA will begin new client engagements by understanding the product and assessing the client's existing quality process before recommending solutions.

The initial approach is:

**Assess → Identify Risks → Prioritize → Recommend → Test → Measure → Improve**

### Future Market

As Gatekeeper QA develops stronger case studies, operational maturity, technical capabilities, and client references, the company may expand its services to larger organizations and enterprises.

## Business Objectives

During its first year, Gatekeeper QA aims to:

1. Build a base of at least 10 active recurring B2B clients by the end of Year 1.

2. Develop an active sales pipeline of at least 15 qualified prospective clients that match Gatekeeper QA's target customer profile.

3. Build client trust and market credibility by obtaining testimonials, references, or documented positive feedback from at least 5 clients.

4. Develop at least 5 strong professional case studies and maintain a portfolio that demonstrates Gatekeeper QA's quality engineering approach, testing evidence, recommendations, and business value.

5. Build toward $35,000 USD in monthly recurring revenue (MRR) by the end of Year 1 through recurring Quality Engineering partnerships and higher-value QA services.

6. Develop professional delivery capability in API testing, CI/CD quality gates, performance testing, mobile testing, and cloud testing while continuing to strengthen Gatekeeper QA's core manual, exploratory, and automation testing capabilities.

7. Establish repeatable QA processes, documentation, reporting, and engineering workflows that allow Gatekeeper QA to grow beyond a founder-only operation.

## Market Positioning

Gatekeeper QA positions itself as an independent Quality Engineering Partner for growing software businesses rather than simply a testing vendor.

Gatekeeper QA complements developers and internal QA teams by providing an additional independent quality perspective focused on product risk, critical user journeys, regression exposure, and release readiness.

Unlike a testing service focused primarily on executing test cases and reporting defects, Gatekeeper QA first seeks to understand the client's product, users, requirements, workflows, and business risks before recommending and executing an appropriate quality strategy.

Gatekeeper QA demonstrates its value through evidence-based quality reports, risk findings, actionable recommendations, and measurable quality improvements where supporting data is available.

The goal is not to maximize the number of defects reported, but to help clients reduce quality risk, prevent recurring problems, improve their quality processes, and make more informed release decisions.

## Core Value Proposition

Gatekeeper QA helps growing software businesses make safer and more informed release decisions by independently assessing their software, identifying quality risks and defects, and providing clear, evidence-based recommendations.

We go beyond simply finding bugs. We seek to understand the client's product, users, critical workflows, and business risks so that testing effort is focused where failures could have the greatest impact.

Through structured quality assessments, testing evidence, risk reporting, and actionable recommendations, Gatekeeper QA helps teams improve release confidence, reduce recurring quality problems, and continuously improve their software quality processes.
